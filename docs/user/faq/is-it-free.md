of [MIT](../../../LICENSE-MIT) or [Apache-2.0](../../../LICENSE-APACHE), with upstream
copyright and provenance preserved. Optional external AI services have their own
access requirements.

separate [branding terms](../../../assets/brand/LICENSE.txt) and
[icon terms](../../../assets/app-icon/LICENSE.txt); open-source code licensing does
not waive those branding rights. Bundled third-party assets retain their own licences.

This is a development preview. See the [development status](../../../PHASE_STATUS.md)
for completed work and remaining gaps.
